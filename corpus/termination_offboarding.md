# Termination and Offboarding Policy

## Overview

This policy covers voluntary resignation, involuntary termination, and end-of-contract separations for all Northwind Robotics employees and contractors.

## Notice Period

Employees resigning voluntarily are requested to provide **2 weeks' notice**. Employees in Level 5 and above roles are requested to provide **4 weeks' notice**. Northwind Robotics may accept a shorter notice period at manager discretion.

The company reserves the right to place a resigning employee on garden leave for the duration of their notice period.

## Involuntary Termination

Involuntary terminations may be for cause or without cause:

- **For cause**: Serious violations of the Code of Conduct, failure to meet a PIP, or gross misconduct. No additional severance is owed beyond final pay and accrued PTO.
- **Without cause**: Layoffs or position eliminations. Severance is provided per the schedule below.

### Severance Schedule (Without-Cause Terminations)

| Tenure | Severance |
|--------|-----------|
| Under 1 year | 4 weeks of base pay |
| 1–3 years | 8 weeks of base pay |
| 3–5 years | 12 weeks of base pay |
| 5+ years | 16 weeks of base pay |

Severance is contingent on signing a separation agreement and general release of claims.

## Final Pay

Final paychecks, including all accrued and unused PTO (up to the 10-day carryover cap as defined in the PTO and Vacation Policy), are issued on the employee's last day or within the timeframe required by applicable state law, whichever is sooner.

## Benefits Continuation

Health, dental, and vision coverage ends at the end of the month in which the last day of employment falls. Employees may continue coverage through COBRA. HR provides COBRA election materials within 5 business days of the separation date.

## System Access Revocation

IT revokes all system access within **4 hours** of the employee's last day, consistent with the Information Security Policy. Employees must return all company equipment (laptops, badges, peripherals) on or before their last day.

## Offboarding Checklist (HR)

- [ ] Separation agreement sent and signed
- [ ] Final paycheck and PTO payout processed
- [ ] IT notified for access revocation (must occur within 4 hours of last day)
- [ ] Company equipment return confirmed
- [ ] Benefits termination and COBRA notice sent
- [ ] RSU and equity status communicated to employee and recorded in cap table
- [ ] Workday profile updated to inactive status
