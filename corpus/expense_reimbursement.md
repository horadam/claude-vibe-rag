# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Overview

Northwind Robotics reimburses employees for reasonable, pre-approved business expenses. All expenses must be submitted within **30 days** of being incurred. Late submissions may be denied.

## Submission Process

1. Collect itemized receipts for all expenses over $25.
2. Submit through Workday under **Expenses > New Expense Report**.
3. Attach receipts as PDF or image files.
4. Select the appropriate expense category and cost center.
5. Manager approval is required for all submissions.

Approved reimbursements are processed within **2 pay cycles** of manager approval.

## Travel

| Category | Policy |
|----------|--------|
| Flights | Economy class required for domestic flights under 6 hours; business class allowed for international or domestic flights over 6 hours with VP approval |
| Hotels | Up to $250/night in most cities; up to $350/night in San Francisco, New York, and Seattle |
| Meals (travel) | Up to $75/day without receipts; full reimbursement with itemized receipts |
| Ground transport | Rideshare, taxi, or public transit; personal vehicle at IRS mileage rate |

## Conferences and Training

Conference registration fees and associated travel are reimbursable with manager approval. Pre-approval is required for any conference expense over $500.

## Home Office Stipend

New remote-eligible employees receive a **one-time $750 home office stipend** for equipment such as monitors, keyboards, and ergonomic accessories. Eligible items must support a productive work-from-home setup. Personal entertainment equipment (TVs, gaming peripherals) is not eligible. Submit through the standard Workday expense process with the category **Home Office Setup**.

## Client Entertainment

Client meals and entertainment are reimbursable with manager approval and require documentation of the business purpose and attendees. The per-person limit for client meals is **$120**. Alcohol is reimbursable up to **$30 per person** at client meals.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

- Personal meals when not traveling
- Alcohol beyond the client entertainment limit
- Fines, tolls from personal commutes, or parking tickets
- Personal subscriptions or software licenses not pre-approved by IT
- Any expense submitted more than 30 days after it was incurred
