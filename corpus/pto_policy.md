# PTO and Vacation Policy

## Overview

Northwind Robotics provides paid time off (PTO) to all full-time employees. PTO covers vacation, personal days, and short-term illness. Part-time employees receive prorated PTO based on their scheduled hours.

## Accrual Rate

Full-time employees accrue **1.5 days of PTO per month**, beginning on their first day of employment. This equals 18 days per calendar year.

Employees in their first 90 days (probationary period) accrue PTO at the standard rate but may not use accrued PTO until the probationary period ends, except in the case of documented medical necessity.

## Accrual Cap

PTO balances are capped at **30 days**. Accrual pauses once an employee reaches the cap and resumes when the balance drops below 30 days.

## PTO Top-Up for Parental Leave

Employees taking parental leave may use accrued PTO to supplement their parental leave pay, extending the total paid period beyond the base parental leave entitlement. See the Parental Leave Policy for base entitlements. The combined paid period (base leave plus PTO top-up) may not exceed 26 weeks.

## Requesting PTO

Employees must submit PTO requests in Workday at least:
- **2 business days** in advance for absences of 1–2 days
- **5 business days** in advance for absences of 3–4 days
- **10 business days** in advance for absences of 5+ days

Manager approval is required. Requests may be denied if operational needs require coverage.

## Carryover

Up to **10 days** of unused PTO may be carried into the following calendar year. Any balance above 10 days is forfeited on January 1.

## PTO Payout at Termination

Upon separation, employees are paid out for any accrued, unused PTO up to the carryover cap (10 days). PTO accrued above the carryover cap that was not used before the separation date is forfeited. See the Termination and Offboarding Policy for the payout timeline.

## Holidays

Northwind Robotics observes **11 company holidays** per year. These are separate from PTO and do not count against accrued balances.
