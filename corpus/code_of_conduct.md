# Code of Conduct

## Overview

Northwind Robotics is committed to maintaining a workplace where every employee is treated with dignity and respect. This Code of Conduct applies to all employees, contractors, and vendors in all work settings — including offices, remote workspaces, company events, and online communication channels.

## Expected Behaviors

Employees are expected to:
- Treat all colleagues, customers, and partners with respect and professionalism
- Communicate honestly and constructively
- Protect confidential company and customer information
- Report conflicts of interest promptly to their manager or HR
- Follow all company policies, including this Code, the Information Security Policy, and the Remote Work Policy

## Prohibited Behaviors

The following are grounds for immediate disciplinary action, up to and including termination:

- Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind
- Falsifying records, timesheets, or expense reports
- Misuse of company assets for personal gain
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential or proprietary information
- Workplace violence or threats
- Repeated or willful violations of the Information Security Policy

## Reporting Concerns

Employees who observe a potential violation should report it through one of the following channels:
- **Direct manager** (if not involved in the concern)
- **HR department** at hr@northwindrobotics.example
- **Anonymous ethics hotline** at ethics.northwindrobotics.example

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is itself a violation of this Code and will be treated as a serious disciplinary matter.

## Investigation Process

All reports are reviewed by HR within 5 business days of receipt. Investigations are conducted confidentially to the extent practicable. Both the reporting employee and the subject of the report will be notified of the outcome.

## Disciplinary Actions

Depending on the severity and circumstances, disciplinary actions may include:
1. Verbal or written warning
2. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
3. Suspension with or without pay
4. Termination

Multiple lesser violations may be treated cumulatively. See the Termination and Offboarding Policy for termination procedures.
