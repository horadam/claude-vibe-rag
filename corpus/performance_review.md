# Performance Review Process

## Overview

Northwind Robotics conducts formal performance reviews twice per year: **mid-year reviews in July** and **annual reviews in January**. The annual review is the primary basis for compensation adjustments and equity refresh grants.

## Review Cycle

| Review | Timing | Purpose |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Mid-year | July 1–15 | Progress check, goal adjustment, early flag of concerns |
| Annual | January 6–20 | Full assessment, compensation decisions, promotion recommendations |

## Self-Assessment

Employees complete a self-assessment in Workday before each review. The self-assessment covers:
- Progress against goals set at the previous review
- Key accomplishments and impact
- Areas for growth
- Goals for the next review period

Self-assessments are due **5 business days before the scheduled review meeting**.

## Manager Assessment

Managers assess employees on five dimensions:
1. **Impact** — Quality and significance of work delivered
2. **Collaboration** — Effectiveness of cross-functional and team partnerships
3. **Communication** — Clarity, frequency, and appropriateness of communication
4. **Growth** — Skill development relative to level expectations
5. **Values alignment** — Adherence to the Code of Conduct and Northwind Robotics principles

Each dimension is scored on a 1–5 scale. A composite score below 2.5 triggers a Performance Improvement Plan discussion.

## Calibration

All manager assessments are reviewed in a calibration session by the department's senior leadership before being finalized. This ensures consistency across teams and levels.

## Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

A PIP is initiated when an employee's composite score falls below 2.5 or when a specific behavioral issue requires structured remediation. PIPs run for **60 days** and include:
- Specific, measurable improvement targets
- Weekly check-ins with the manager
- A formal review at the 30-day and 60-day marks

If performance meets targets by the end of the PIP, the PIP is closed and noted in the employee's record. If not, the employee may be placed on an extended PIP or separated. See the Termination and Offboarding Policy for separation procedures.

## Promotion Recommendations

Promotion recommendations are submitted during the annual review cycle. Promotions require:
- A composite score of 4.0 or higher in the current cycle
- Manager endorsement and skip-level approval
- Confirmation that the employee meets the competency expectations for the target level
