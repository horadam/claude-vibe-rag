# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Overview

Northwind Robotics supports flexible work arrangements. Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week** with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval and are reviewed annually.

## Eligibility

Remote and hybrid work is available to employees who:
- Have completed their 90-day probationary period
- Are in good standing (no active performance improvement plan)
- Have a role that can be performed effectively without daily on-site presence

Hardware engineering and lab roles require on-site presence by default. Exceptions require director approval.

## Home Office Requirements

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a workspace that supports productive, professional work. Northwind Robotics provides a **one-time home office stipend of $750** for new remote-eligible employees, paid as a reimbursement after receipt submission. See the Expense Reimbursement Policy for submission details.

## Information Security Requirements

Remote employees must comply with all requirements in the **Information Security Policy**, including:
- Using only company-issued or approved devices for work
- Connecting through the company VPN whenever accessing internal systems
- Not working from public networks without VPN active
- Keeping workstation screens locked when unattended

Failure to follow information security requirements while working remotely may result in loss of remote work privileges. Repeated violations are subject to disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct.

## Core Hours

All employees, regardless of location, are expected to be available and responsive during **core hours of 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM Pacific Time** on business days.

## Equipment

Company-issued laptops and peripherals are provided to all employees. Employees may not use personal devices to access production systems or internal source code repositories. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported within 24 hours to IT.

## International Remote Work

Working remotely from outside the United States requires advance approval from HR and Legal. Short stays (under 14 consecutive days) require manager approval only. Stays of 14 days or longer trigger tax and employment-law review.
