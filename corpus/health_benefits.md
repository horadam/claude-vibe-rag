# Health and Dental Benefits

## Overview

Northwind Robotics provides comprehensive health and dental coverage to all full-time employees and their eligible dependents. Coverage begins on the **first day of the month following the employee's start date**.

## Medical Plans

Employees may choose from three medical plans during open enrollment:

| Plan | Monthly Premium (Employee) | Deductible | Out-of-Pocket Max |
|------|---------------------------|------------|-------------------|
| Gold PPO | $85 | $500 | $3,000 |
| Silver HMO | $40 | $1,200 | $5,000 |
| Bronze HDHP | $0 | $2,800 | $7,000 |

Northwind Robotics covers **100% of the employee premium** for the Silver HMO plan. For other plans, the company covers the Silver HMO equivalent premium and the employee pays the difference.

The Bronze HDHP is paired with a **Health Savings Account (HSA)**. The company contributes **$600 per year** to the HSA for enrolled employees.

## Dental Coverage

A single dental plan is offered:
- **Preventive care**: 100% covered (two cleanings per year, X-rays)
- **Basic restorative** (fillings, extractions): 80% covered after a $50 deductible
- **Major restorative** (crowns, bridges): 50% covered after a $50 deductible
- **Orthodontia**: 50% covered, lifetime maximum of $2,000

## Vision Coverage

Basic vision coverage is included at no additional cost:
- Annual eye exam: 100% covered
- Frames or contacts: up to $150 allowance per year

## Dependent Coverage

Employees may add spouses, domestic partners, and children (up to age 26) to their plan. Dependent premiums are the employee's responsibility. See HR for current dependent premium rates.

## Benefits During Leave

Health, dental, and vision benefits continue during paid leave periods, including paid parental leave. During unpaid leave, employees may continue coverage by paying the full premium (employer + employee share) through COBRA or a direct-pay arrangement with HR.

## Open Enrollment

Open enrollment occurs each November for coverage beginning January 1. Qualifying life events (marriage, birth, loss of other coverage) allow mid-year enrollment changes within 30 days of the event.
