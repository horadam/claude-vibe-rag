# Parental Leave Policy

## Overview

Northwind Robotics supports employees welcoming a new child through birth, adoption, or foster placement. This policy covers all full-time employees who have completed at least 6 months of continuous employment.

## Base Parental Leave Entitlement

The **primary caregiver** receives **16 weeks of paid parental leave** at 100% of base salary, beginning on the date of birth, adoption finalization, or foster placement.

The **secondary caregiver** receives **6 weeks of paid parental leave** at 100% of base salary.

Leave must be taken as a continuous block unless a return-to-work agreement is approved by HR. Intermittent parental leave is not available under this policy.

## PTO Top-Up

Employees may extend their paid parental leave period by using accrued PTO. The PTO and Vacation Policy governs accrual and eligibility. The total combined paid period (base parental leave plus PTO top-up) may not exceed **26 weeks** for the primary caregiver or **10 weeks** for the secondary caregiver.

## Leave for Adoption and Foster Placement

Employees adopting a child or receiving a foster placement receive the same base entitlements as birth parents. Reasonable paid time off (up to 3 days, drawn from PTO) is also provided for required pre-placement appointments.

## Benefits During Leave

Health, dental, and vision benefits continue uninterrupted during paid parental leave. Equity vesting continues during paid leave periods. See the Health and Dental Benefits Policy for plan details.

## Return to Work

Employees returning from parental leave are entitled to return to the same or an equivalent role. Managers must initiate a re-onboarding plan at least 2 weeks before the employee's scheduled return date.

## Notice Requirements

Employees should notify HR and their manager at least **8 weeks** before the expected leave start date. For unexpected events (premature birth, emergency foster placement), notification must occur as soon as practicable.
