# Compensation and Equity

## Overview

Northwind Robotics aims to offer compensation in the **65th–75th percentile** of market rates for each role and level, benchmarked annually against Radford and Levels.fyi data. Total compensation includes base salary, performance bonus, and equity.

## Base Salary

Base salaries are reviewed annually during the January performance review cycle. Merit increases are based on performance review scores:

| Composite Score | Merit Increase |
|----------------|----------------|
| 4.5 – 5.0 | 8–12% |
| 3.5 – 4.4 | 4–7% |
| 2.5 – 3.4 | 0–3% |
| Below 2.5 | 0% (PIP required) |

Out-of-cycle adjustments require VP approval and are reserved for significant market corrections or role changes.

## Performance Bonus

Employees at Level 4 and above are eligible for an annual performance bonus. The target bonus is:
- Level 4: 10% of base salary
- Level 5: 15% of base salary
- Level 6 and above: 20%+ (determined by the compensation committee)

Bonus payouts depend on company performance (50% weight) and individual performance score (50% weight). Employees must be employed on the bonus payment date to receive the payout.

## Equity

### Initial Grant

New hires receive a Restricted Stock Unit (RSU) grant, sized based on level:
- Level 3: $80,000 in RSUs
- Level 4: $160,000 in RSUs
- Level 5: $300,000 in RSUs
- Level 6+: Determined individually

RSUs vest over **4 years** with a **1-year cliff**: 25% vests after 12 months, then 1/48th per month for the remaining 36 months.

### Refresh Grants

Annual refresh grants are awarded during the January review cycle to employees with a composite score of 3.5 or higher. Refresh grant sizes are at manager and VP discretion, typically 25–50% of the initial grant value.

Equity vesting continues during paid leave (including paid parental leave) and pauses during unpaid leave exceeding 30 days.

## Pay Transparency

Employees are encouraged to discuss their compensation with colleagues. Northwind Robotics does not prohibit compensation discussions and will not retaliate against employees who share salary information.

## Payroll

Employees are paid bi-weekly (26 pay periods per year) via direct deposit. Paystubs are available in Workday.
